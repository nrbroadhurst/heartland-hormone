## 3. Compounded formulation, if that box is checked

A compounding pharmacy prepares this medicine for me. It is not the commercially manufactured pen or vial sold as an FDA-approved product.

A compounded preparation is not an FDA-approved drug product. The FDA does not review that preparation for safety, effectiveness, or quality in the way it reviews an approved medicine.

FDA-approved medicines in this class are available for specific uses, which can include type 2 diabetes or chronic weight management, depending on the product. A compounded preparation can differ from an approved medicine, and from one batch to another, in strength, ingredients, sterility, and how the dose is measured.

Some compounded preparations use a different chemical form than the ingredient in the FDA-approved medicine. My provider has told me the form and concentration in my prescription. I will use only the amount written on my pharmacy label. I will measure each dose with the syringe or device the pharmacy provides. I will not convert the dose on my own. Using the wrong amount can cause serious harm.

If I want a commercial FDA-approved product and one is appropriate, my provider can prescribe that instead.

## 4. Purpose

This medicine is being considered for weight management or another metabolic reason my provider has explained, based on my history, examination when indicated, and laboratory results.

No amount of weight loss has been promised. Weight, appetite, blood sugar, and other results may not change. Weight may return if the medicine is stopped.

## 5. Evaluation and monitoring

I understand this medicine is offered only after a clinical evaluation. My provider may repeat visits and laboratory tests while I am treated and may change or stop the medicine based on my response, side effects, or results.

I will tell my provider about all medicines and supplements I use, including insulin and other diabetes medicines. I will tell my provider if I am pregnant, may be pregnant, planning pregnancy, or breastfeeding. This medicine is not used in pregnancy. My provider will tell me how long to stop it before a planned pregnancy.

I will tell my provider if I or a family member has had medullary thyroid cancer, if I have Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2, or if I have had pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, severe stomach or intestinal disease, gastroparesis, kidney disease, diabetic eye disease, type 1 diabetes, or a serious reaction to a medicine in this class.

I will tell my provider before surgery or a procedure that uses sedation, because this medicine slows emptying of the stomach.

## 6. Risks

Risks that have been explained to me include:

- Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, abdominal pain, or loss of appetite
- Dehydration or a change in kidney function if I cannot keep fluids down
- Gallbladder disease, including gallstones
- Pancreatitis, which can be severe. I will stop the medicine and get urgent care for severe abdominal pain that will not go away, with or without vomiting
- Low blood sugar, especially if I also use insulin or a sulfonylurea
- A change in diabetic eye disease
- Thyroid C-cell tumors have been found in animal studies of FDA-approved medicines in this class. It is not known whether these medicines cause those tumors in people. This medicine is not used if I or a family member has had medullary thyroid cancer, or if I have Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2
- Mood changes. I will tell my provider if I have new or worsening depression or thoughts of harming myself
- Delayed stomach emptying, which can matter for procedures that use sedation
- Pain, redness, bruising, or infection at an injection site
- Allergic reaction, including rash, trouble breathing, or a severe reaction that needs emergency care
- Differences in a compounded preparation from batch to batch, and harm from taking the wrong dose of a compounded product
- Unknown or uncommon effects, including effects that are still being studied

This list does not include every possible effect. My provider has discussed the risks that apply to the medicine named above and to my own history.

## 7. Alternatives

My provider has discussed alternatives, including no medicine, an FDA-approved medicine when one is appropriate, a different prescription, nutrition and activity changes, and referral for other medical or surgical weight treatment. I may choose an alternative or decline this medicine.

## 8. My responsibilities

I will use this medicine only as prescribed. I will not share it, change the dose on my own, or substitute a product from another source. I will get it from the pharmacy my provider uses. I will store and dispose of it as the pharmacy and my provider instruct.

I will contact the clinic about side effects that concern me. I will seek emergency care for severe abdominal pain, trouble breathing, chest pain, fainting, signs of a severe allergic reaction, or symptoms of very low blood sugar that I cannot correct.

I will keep follow-up visits and complete laboratory tests my provider orders.

## 9. Right to refuse or stop

I may refuse this treatment or stop it later. Stopping may mean my appetite and weight return toward where they were. I will tell the clinic if I stop so my record and any monitoring can be updated.

Signing this form does not require me to start or continue treatment.
