## 3. Purpose

Peptide therapy uses a prescribed peptide to address a specific health goal discussed with my provider. Possible goals may include support of body composition, recovery, metabolic health, or sexual health, depending on the peptide and my evaluation.

My provider has explained why this peptide is being considered for me, what change is hoped for, and how we will decide whether to continue it. No specific result has been promised. People respond differently. Symptoms, laboratory results, and body composition may not change.

## 4. Evaluation and monitoring

I understand that peptide therapy is offered only after a clinical evaluation. My provider may review my history, medicines, examination findings, and laboratory results before prescribing, and may repeat laboratory tests or visits while I am treated.

I will tell my provider about all prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, and supplements I use. I will tell my provider if I am pregnant, trying to become pregnant, or breastfeeding. I understand peptide therapy is not offered during pregnancy or breastfeeding unless my provider specifically tells me otherwise for a particular product.

I will tell my provider about any history of cancer, tumor, allergy, or serious reaction to an injection or a medicine.

## 5. Risks

Risks that have been explained to me include:

- Pain, redness, swelling, bruising, itching, or infection at an injection site
- Allergic reaction, including rash, trouble breathing, or a severe reaction that needs emergency care
- Headache, flushing, dizziness, nausea, fatigue, or fluid retention
- Changes in blood sugar, blood pressure, heart rate, or other hormone levels
- Interaction with my other medicines or health conditions
- Differences in compounded products from batch to batch, including strength or inactive ingredients
- Effects that are not yet well studied, including possible long-term effects
- For growth-hormone–related peptides, a possible concern in people with active cancer or unexplained tumors, which my provider has considered

This list does not include every possible effect. My provider has discussed the risks that matter for the peptide named above and for my own history.

## 6. Alternatives

My provider has discussed alternatives, including:

- No peptide therapy
- Other prescription treatment, if one is appropriate
- Lifestyle measures such as nutrition, activity, and sleep
- Treating the concern without this peptide

I may choose an alternative or decline peptide therapy.

## 7. My responsibilities

I will use the peptide only as prescribed. I will not share it, change the dose on my own, or substitute a product from another source.

I will get the medicine from the pharmacy my provider uses. I will store and dispose of it as the pharmacy and my provider instruct.

I will contact the clinic about side effects that concern me. I will seek emergency care for severe symptoms such as trouble breathing, chest pain, fainting, or a rapidly worsening reaction.

I will keep follow-up visits and complete laboratory tests my provider orders. My provider may stop or change treatment based on my response, side effects, or test results.

## 8. Right to refuse or stop

I may refuse this treatment or stop it later. Stopping may mean the condition being treated stays the same or returns. I will tell the clinic if I stop so my record and any monitoring can be updated.

Signing this form does not require me to start or continue treatment.
